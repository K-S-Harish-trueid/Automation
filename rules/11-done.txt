STEP 11 -- done ("Final Output")
================================================================================
TYPE      done (terminal state, nothing left to run)
CODE      backend/app/routes/jobs.py :: download_final, download_audit
          backend/app/helpers.py :: _write_flat_xlsx

WHAT IT DOES
Nothing further happens to the data here -- it's exactly what step 10
(default_id) produced. This step just exposes the two downloads.

CONSTRAINTS
  1. No transformation, validation, or flagging happens at this step.
  2. FINAL DOWNLOAD -- GET /jobs/{id}/download returns
     "{job_id}_final.xlsx": one flat sheet named "Final Output", every
     column in the working DataFrame, same shape as the original raw
     import (no splitting by topic -- there's nothing left to review by
     this point).
  3. AUDIT DOWNLOAD -- GET /jobs/{id}/audit/download returns
     "{job_id}_audit.xlsx": every field-level change made across every
     prior step (source file, operator, reason, old/new value, timestamp),
     independent of the final dataset.
  4. RETENTION -- once a job reaches "done" it counts against the
     configured cap of most-recently-completed jobs kept on disk; older
     completed jobs beyond that cap (and their downloadable files) are
     deleted automatically. Jobs still waiting on a manual gate are never
     pruned by this rule.

EDITABLE FIELDS
None -- terminal state.
