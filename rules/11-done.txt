STEP 11 - done ("Final Output")
Type: done (terminal state, nothing left to run)
Source: backend/app/routes/jobs.py :: download_final, download_audit

RULES
1. No further transformation of the data happens at this step -- the
   DataFrame is exactly what step 10 (default_id) produced.
2. The final dataset is downloadable as an .xlsx file named "jod.xlsx"
   (GET /jobs/{id}/download); every column present in the working DataFrame
   is written out, in its current column order.
3. A separate audit trail of every field-level change made across every
   prior step (source file, operator, reason, old/new value, timestamp) can
   be downloaded independently as an .xlsx via GET /jobs/{id}/audit/download.
4. Per job retention rules, once a job reaches "done" it counts against the
   configured cap of most-recently-completed jobs kept on disk -- older
   completed jobs beyond that cap (and their downloadable files) are deleted
   automatically. Jobs still waiting on a manual gate are never pruned.
