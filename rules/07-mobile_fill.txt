STEP 7 - mobile_fill ("Missing Mobile Numbers")
Type: manual_edit (pages 200 flagged rows at a time)
Source: backend/app/pipeline/validators.py :: mask_mobile_missing
         backend/app/pipeline/reasons.py :: validation_reasons_mobile

FLAGGING RULE
A row is flagged if PHONE_NUMBER equals the sentinel value
XXX_NOT_COLLECTED_XXX, or is blank/empty. (Step 6 sets this sentinel for
any phone number that was blank or all-zeros, so in practice this step
catches whatever step 6 could not fill in.)

REASON MESSAGE
"Phone number is missing."

EDITABLE FIELDS
PHONE_NUMBER.

CONTEXT COLUMNS SHOWN (read-only)
ACCOUNT_NUMBER, ACCOUNT_FIRST_NAME, ACCOUNT_LAST_NAME.

ADVANCE RULE
Re-validates after every batch. Advances once zero rows remain flagged, or
immediately on force_advance=true (rows left with a missing/sentinel phone
number are not revisited later in the pipeline).
